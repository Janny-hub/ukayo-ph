Order Processing Fee (₱5.00 Flat) Effective Policy

The Order Processing Fee is a fixed platform infrastructure charge dedicated to funding the continuous engineering, server hosting, security upkeep, and feature upgrades of the UKAYO marketplace—ensuring fast, reliable live-selling and checkout performance for both miners and sellers.

It powers core merchant capabilities including live rack management, automated Mine Na! locking, J&T A6 waybill generation, real-time payout tracking, and dispute protection. UKAYO charges a flat ₱5.00 fee per successfully completed order (in addition to the 6.00% flat commission). Cancelled and fully returned/refunded orders are exempt from this ₱5.00 fee.

Part I: Onboarding, KYC & BIR RR 16-2023
  • • Primary ID & Selfie KYC: Valid Philippine Government Primary ID (PhilSys National ID, Passport, LTO Driver's License, UMID, PRC, or Postal ID) + live selfie required before going live.
  • • BIR Form 2303 (COR): Upload your existing DTI & BIR Form 2303 in Seller Center (no duplicate registration required if already registered on Shopee/TikTok).
  • • Withholding Tax (BIR RR 16-2023): 1% on one-half (0.5%) of net item price is withheld only if annual gross remittances exceed ₱500,000 or no BIR-received Sworn Declaration is on file.
Part II: Listings, Mine Na! Live & Prohibited Items
  • • Measurements & Flaw Disclosure: State exact flat-lay width × length (inches/cm) and disclose any stains, pinholes, or wash fade during live streams or feed posts.
  • • Zero Counterfeit Safe-Harbor: Authentic secondhand/ukay garments and unbranded bales are protected; bootleg/counterfeit replicas are strictly banned.
  • • J&T Prohibited Goods: Aerosols, perfumes, flammables, liquids, cash, jewelry, and sharp objects are banned from UKAYO parcels.
Part III: J&T Express Shipping & Fulfillment SLA
  • • 12-Hour Checkout Rule: Buyers locking an item via Mine Na! have 12 hours to pay; unpaid items auto-unlock to the next Steal queue miner.
  • • 2-Business-Day Ship-Out: Print the A6 thermal waybill (105 × 148 mm) in solid black ink (do not tape over the barcode) and hand over to J&T within 2 business days.
  • • Weight & Size Limits: Up to 8 kg for J&T pouches and 50 kg maximum per parcel (150 cm max side) for wholesale bales.
Part IV: Preloved Returns, Refunds & Disputes
  • • No Change-of-Mind Returns: Preloved ukay items are one-of-a-kind; change-of-mind returns or minor vintage wear disclosed on live are not valid refund grounds.
  • • 48-Hour Dispute Window: Buyers may file a dispute within 48 hours of delivery with an unedited unboxing video only for wrong item, missing item, or major undisclosed damage.
  • • RTS Protection: Valid seller-error returns deduct return freight from seller balance; buyer COD refusals incur buyer strikes after 2 RTS incidents.
Part V: Seller Payouts, Vouchers & Sample Math
  • • Twice-Weekly Settlement: Escrow releases after J&T delivery + 48-hour inspection. Automatic payouts run every Tuesday & Friday to GCash, Maya, GoTyme, or Bank (₱0 fee ≥ ₱500; ₱15 fee below ₱500).
  • • Seller-Sponsored Vouchers: Optional shipping or shop discount vouchers created in Seller Center are 100% funded by the seller and deducted from payout.
  • • ₱299 Hoodie (+₱85 Ship): ₱17.94 (6% comm) + ₱5.00 (Order Processing Fee) → ₱276.06 COD or ₱267.50 Bayad Agad (after ₱8.56 gateway fee).
  • • ₱4,000 Bale (+₱350 Ship): ₱240.00 (6% comm) + ₱5.00 (Order Processing Fee) → ₱3,755.00 COD or ₱3,658.00 Bayad Agad (after ₱97.00 gateway fee).
Part VI: Seller Penalty Point System (Quarterly Reset)
  • • Points: +1 pt late shipment (>2 business days), +1 pt seller cancellation (>5% rate), +3 to +6 pts counterfeit/prohibited item.
  • • Tiers: 0–2 pts (Full access + Top Seller badge) • 3–5 pts (7-day Home Live banner pause) • 6+ pts (14-day live/COD suspension). Resets every quarter.
